Lookalike audiences are not a magic scaling lever. They are a way to help the
platform find people who resemble a useful source group. That means the quality
of the seed matters more than the label on the campaign.

## Build better seeds

Purchase value, retention, repeat purchase, high-intent lead quality, and
category-specific behavior can all create stronger seed audiences than broad
traffic lists. A small but qualified seed often beats a larger low-signal list.

## Pair audiences with creative angles

Scaling fails when the audience gets broader but the creative stays generic.
For each audience expansion, I want creative that makes the product promise
specific enough for a new group to understand quickly.

## Watch the signal

When CPA rises, I check whether the issue is audience quality, creative fatigue,
landing page conversion, or tracking noise. The answer decides whether to widen,
refresh, rebuild, or stop.
